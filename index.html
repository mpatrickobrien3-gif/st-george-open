Sand Hollow · Hurricane, Utah
Friday · August 14
Sand Hollow
2 vs. 2 aggregate score match play. Add both partners’ scores on each hole and compare the totals.

Course Rules

  • One point for the front nine, one for the back nine, and one for the overall match.
  • Tied holes do not carry over or stack.
  • Each team gets one Barney Ball for tee-shot mulligans. Once used, it must finish the hole.
  • If the Barney Ball may be lost, the team has three minutes to find it or its mulligan powers are gone.
🎱 Barney Ball Returns
The Ledges · St. George, Utah
Saturday · August 15
The Ledges
The gimmick round. Final format pending the group vote: front-nine scramble/back-nine alternate shot or an 18-hole scramble.

Course Rules

  • One point for the front nine, one for the back nine, and one for the overall match.
  • Each team may use one reverse mulligan on any opposing drive, approach, chip, or putt.
  • Each player may choose one hole to play from the forward tees.
  • Partners cannot use the forward-tee benefit on the same hole.
↩️ Reverse Mulligan
Wolf Creek · Mesquite, Nevada
Sunday · August 16
Wolf Creek
2 vs. 2 best ball. Only the lower score between the two partners counts on each hole.

Course Rules

  • One point for the front nine, one for the back nine, and one for the overall match.
  • Pairings follow draft order: picks 1–2, 3–4, and 5–6 play together.
  • Equivalent draft-order pairings face one another across the two teams.
  • The duo delivering the biggest beatdown of the day earns double points.
⚡ Double-Point Beatdown

Official-ish Handbook

Rules of Engagement

No handicap adjustments. No repeated pairings. No sympathy putts unless the opposition gives them.

No Handicaps

All formats are played straight up. No match or score is adjusted for handicap.

Team Draft

Glaz and Coombs captain two teams of six. The draft is snake style, with Glaz choosing whether to select first or second.

Friday & Saturday Pairings

Captains submit A, B, and C pairings for Sand Hollow and The Ledges. A, B, and C do not need to correspond to skill level.

Captain Pairing Restriction

For Friday and Saturday, a captain cannot pair himself with his first or second draft pick. No two-player pairing may repeat during the trip.

Gimmes

All putts must be holed unless the opposing team explicitly concedes the putt.

Out of Bounds

Red stakes use normal lateral-hazard relief. For white stakes, replay from the tee hitting three or take lateral relief and play the next shot as four.

*Point totals and final winning threshold will be confirmed once the Ledges format and Wolf Creek bonus math are finalized.

Prize & Punishment

🏆 Prize

A Wolf Creek or Sand Hollow golf hat that will serve as the official St George Open trophy.

💀 Punishment

The losing team must wear a 1920s-inspired golf outfit for the next round of golf they play after the trip. The round may be played solo or with a group, but the outfit must be worn throughout the full golf experience, including checking in at the clubhouse, warming up on the range, and stopping for food or a drink at the turn. The goal is to make sure fellow golfers and course staff get the full experience.
